Elm Street Bike Co-op

Gallery

Elm Street Bike Co-op is a volunteer-run nonprofit shop where neighbors bring in old bikes, fix them up together, and give the finished bikes to anyone in the neighborhood who needs one. This page shows some of our recent work.
